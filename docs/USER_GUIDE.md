# Studify User Guide

**For:** anyone using Studify, on any Spotify plan, on a computer or a phone.
**Setting Studify up yourself?** Use the [Setup Guide](./SPOTIFY_SETUP.md) instead. **Not sure which guide you need?** See [Start here](./README.md).

Studify is a study companion for Spotify:
- the song that's playing appears as a spinning vinyl record;
- the screen takes its colours from the album cover;
- your playlists and albums sit on a shelf of records;
- the lyrics scroll along with the song;
- a focus timer helps you study in blocks.

---

## Quick start (4 steps)

1. **Open Studify** at the address the person who runs it gave you.
2. Click **Connect Spotify** (top right), then **Agree** on Spotify's page.
3. **Open the Spotify app** on your phone or computer. You don't need to play anything in it.
4. In Studify, pick a record on the shelf and press **Play**. *(Premium. On Spotify Free, play music in the Spotify app and Studify follows along.)*

That's it. Everything else in this guide is detail you can look up when you need it.

---

## Known problems right now

These are being worked on. They come from changes Spotify made for apps in 2026.

- **Spotify no longer tells Studify whether you have Free or Premium.** Studify now assumes Premium. A **Free** account sees one message the first time it presses play (*"This account is on Spotify Free…"*). After that, Studify switches to follow mode by itself and remembers it until you disconnect.
- **Song lists open only for playlists you made or collaborate on.** Spotify no longer shares the songs of playlists you only follow. You can still **play** any playlist from the shelf, and albums and liked songs work normally. A playlist without a song count from Spotify shows no count, rather than a wrong "0 tracks".

# Getting started

## 1. What you need

- **A Spotify account**, Free or Premium. See the next section for the difference.
- **The Spotify app** on any device: your phone, your computer, or a speaker. Usually the music plays from the Spotify app, and Studify shows it and (with Premium) controls it.
- **A web browser**: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer or a phone.
- **Permission to use Studify.** Spotify keeps apps like Studify private. The person who runs Studify must add your Spotify email address first, and Spotify allows **at most 5 people** per Studify. If connecting fails, ask them to add you.

## 2. Free or Premium: what works

Spotify only lets apps **control** music for Premium accounts. Free accounts can still use most of Studify. You just press play, pause and skip in the Spotify app instead.

| Feature | Free | Premium |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| See what's playing, with the spinning record | ✅ | ✅ |
| Colours that follow the album cover | ✅ | ✅ |
| Song progress bar | ✅ (watch only) | ✅ (drag to jump) |
| Play, pause, next, previous from Studify | Use the Spotify app | ✅ |
| Browse your playlists, albums and liked songs | ✅ | ✅ |
| Start a playlist or album from the record shelf | Use the Spotify app | ✅ |
| See the songs in a playlist or album | ✅ | ✅ |
| Start a specific song from a list | Use the Spotify app | ✅ |
| Lyrics that scroll with the song | ✅ | ✅ (tap a line to jump to it) |
| **Play here**: play the music inside the Studify tab (computers only) | ❌ | ✅ |
| Focus timer | ✅ | ✅ |

> **Tip for Free accounts:** keep the Spotify app open next to Studify. Whatever you play there appears in Studify within a couple of seconds.

## 3. Connecting your Spotify account

You only do this once per browser.

1. Open Studify.
2. Click the green **Connect Spotify** button in the **top-right corner**.
3. Your browser may ask whether Studify can **show notifications**. Choose **Allow** if you want a notification when a study session ends. If you say no, Studify shows a pop-up instead.
4. Spotify's login page opens. Log in if asked.
5. Spotify lists what Studify wants to see and do. Click **Agree**.
6. You're sent back to Studify. The top-right now says **● Spotify connected**.

**What Studify asks Spotify for, and why**

| Permission | Why Studify needs it |
|---|---|
| See what you're playing | To show the song, cover and progress |
| Control playback | For the play, pause, skip and seek buttons (Premium only) |
| Read your playlists, saved albums and liked songs | To fill the record shelf and the track lists |
| See your account details | To show the right controls for your plan |
| Play music in the browser | For **Play here** (Premium, computers) |

Studify can't change your playlists, follow anyone, or see your password.

**Asked to "Reconnect Spotify"?** When a new Studify feature needs a new permission, people who connected earlier see a **Reconnect Spotify** button. Click it and agree again. Nothing else changes.

## 4. A tour of the screen

On a computer you see **two columns** side by side. On a phone they're **stacked**: the player first, then the right column below it.

**Top bar**
- **STUDIFY**: the name.
- **Connect Spotify** (before connecting) or **● Spotify connected** (after). Click the green-dot button to disconnect.

**Left column: Now playing**
- **Focus timer** button: opens the study timer (see [section 8](#8-the-focus-timer)). While a session runs, it shows the time left, for example **Focus · 18:42**.
- **The album cover** with its **record**. The record slides out of the cover when a song is loaded and spins while it plays. It slows to a stop when you pause, like a real turntable.
- Click the **cover** to open the song in Spotify.
- **Song name** and **artist · album**.
- **Progress bar**, with the time played and the song length.
- **⏮ Previous · ▶ Play / ⏸ Pause · ⏭ Next**.
- **Play here** (Premium, computers): see [section 5](#5-playing-music).

**Right column: three tabs, Records · Tracks · Lyrics.** The big title changes to match.
- **Records**: your music. **Playlists · Albums · Liked**, the **record shelf**, and a bar with **Tracks ›** and **Play** (see [section 6](#6-your-records-the-record-shelf)).
- **Tracks**: the songs in the playlist or album playing now (see [section 7](#7-tracks-and-lyrics)).
- **Lyrics**: the words of the song, scrolling along with it (see [section 7](#7-tracks-and-lyrics)).

**The colours change with the music.** The background, panels and buttons take their colour from the album cover. They fade smoothly to new colours when the song changes, and text always stays readable.

# Using Studify

## 5. Playing music

**Starting music from Studify (Premium)**
1. Make sure the **Spotify app is open** on your phone or computer. You **don't** have to play anything in it.
2. In Studify, press **Play** on a record (or click a song in a list).
3. The music starts in the Spotify app, and Studify shows it.

If you have Spotify open on more than one device, Studify picks the one like the device you're using: your phone's app when you're on your phone, the computer app when you're on a computer.

**Controls (Premium)**
- **▶ / ⏸**: play or pause. Pressed when nothing is chosen, it continues whatever Spotify played last.
- **⏮ / ⏭**: previous or next song.
- **Progress bar**: drag the dot, or click anywhere on the bar, to jump to that point.

**Free accounts:** play, pause and skip in the Spotify app. Studify follows along. If you press a Studify button you'll see *"Playback control requires Spotify Premium."*

**How fast Studify follows the Spotify app:** within about **2 seconds**, or every second while the lyrics are open. Your own button presses in Studify show up almost immediately.

**Play here (Premium, computers)**

*See [Known problems](#known-problems-right-now): this button is temporarily hidden.*

Normally the music plays from the Spotify app. With **Play here**, the Studify tab itself becomes the player:
1. Click **Play here** under the play button.
2. The first time, Spotify may ask for one more permission. Click **Agree**.
3. The music moves to the Studify tab, and the button reads **Playing in this browser**.

Why use it? The lyrics line up best this way. To move the music back, pick another device in the Spotify app (the **Connect to a device** icon). *Play here* doesn't work in phone browsers, because Spotify doesn't support it there.

## 6. Your records: the record shelf

The shelf shows your music as records seen from the side. Each record's **spine** shows its name and is coloured like its cover. The record in the **middle** (between the two coloured markers) is the **selected** one.

**Browse**
- **Mouse or trackpad:** scroll over the shelf. It settles on one record at a time.
- **Touch:** swipe up or down on the shelf.
- **Keyboard:** see [section 10](#10-keyboard-shortcuts).
- Use the **Playlists**, **Albums** and **Liked** tabs above the shelf to switch.

**Select and play**
1. **Tap or click a record** to bring it to the middle.
2. Its name and details appear in the bar underneath.
3. Press **Play** (or tap the middle record again). *(Premium. On Free, the button reads **Open in Spotify** and opens the record there.)*

**See the songs first: Tracks ›**
1. Select a playlist or album.
2. Press **Tracks ›** in the bar underneath. Its songs slide in over the shelf.
3. Click a song to start the record **from that song**. *(Premium. On Free, it opens the song in Spotify.)*
4. Press **‹** (top left of the list) or **Esc** to slide back to the shelf.

**What gets played**
- **Playlist or album:** plays from the start and carries on through it.
- **Liked song:** plays that song, then carries on through the rest of the liked songs on the shelf.

**What you might see on the shelf**

| You see | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Grey records | Your music is loading | Wait a moment |
| *Connect Spotify to browse…* | You're not connected | Click **Connect Spotify** (top right) |
| *Studify needs permission to read your library* | You connected before the shelf existed | Click **Reconnect Spotify** |
| *Couldn't load your records* | A network or Spotify problem | Click **Try again** |
| *No playlists / albums / liked songs yet* | That tab is empty in your Spotify | Save some music in Spotify |

> The shelf shows your **first 50** playlists, albums or liked songs in each tab.

## 7. Tracks and lyrics

### The Tracks tab
Shows the songs in the **playlist or album that's playing right now**, and follows along as the music changes.
- The playing song has a small **equaliser icon** and coloured text.
- **Click a song** to jump to it. *(Premium. On Free, it opens in Spotify.)*
- Long playlists load more songs as you scroll down.
- **Greyed-out songs** can't be played from apps. These are local files, or songs not available in your country.
- *"Nothing playing from a list"* means the music isn't coming from a playlist or album, for example an artist radio.

### The Lyrics tab
The words of the song, **scrolling with the music**, like in Spotify.
- The line being sung is **bright**. Lines already sung are dimmer. Lines still to come are faint.
- **Click a line** to jump the song to that moment. *(Premium)*
- **Scroll the lyrics yourself** any time. Studify stops following for a few seconds and shows **Back to current line**. Click it, or wait, to catch up.
- Songs **without timings** show their words as plain text, labelled *"not synced"*.
- *"No lyrics for this song yet"* means the lyrics library doesn't have this song. *"Instrumental"* means the song has no words.
- Lyrics come from **LRCLIB**, a free lyrics library made by volunteers. Some songs are missing, and a few may be slightly off.

**How closely the lyrics follow the song**

| How you're listening | The lyrics are off by at most about |
|---|---|
| **Play here** (Premium, computer) | a twentieth of a second |
| The Spotify app on a phone, computer or speaker (any plan) | a third to half a second |

### When Studify switches to the lyrics by itself
When **you** start a record or a song from Studify, the shelf **folds away** and the **Lyrics** tab opens in its place (or **Tracks**, if the song has no lyrics). It only happens:
- right after **you** pressed play in Studify, never just because the song changed;
- if you haven't clicked anything else in that column since.

Don't want it? Turn off **Show lyrics when I play** at the bottom of the Lyrics tab. To go back to your records, click the **Records** tab.

## 8. The focus timer

The timer helps you study in focused blocks with short breaks (often called the *Pomodoro* method). It's **hidden until you need it**.

**Open and close**
- Click **Focus timer** at the top of the player. A panel slides up.
- Close it with **✕**, the **Esc** key, or by clicking **Focus timer** again. The timer keeps running while the panel is closed.

**Choose your session lengths**
- **Focus:** 5 to 90 minutes (in 5-minute steps).
- **Break:** 1 to 30 minutes.
- Drag a slider, or click it and use the arrow keys.
- Studify **remembers your lengths** in this browser.
- You can only change the lengths while the timer is **paused**. Changing a length restarts the current session at the new length.

**Run it**
- **Start / Pause**: start or pause the countdown (keyboard: **T**).
- **↺ Reset**: back to the full length of the current session.
- **» Skip**: jump straight to the break, or back to focus.

**While it runs**
- The **Focus timer** button shows the time left, for example **Focus · 18:42**.
- The **browser tab title** shows it too, so you can see it from another tab.
- **Focus** is shown in coral with a ✎ pencil. **Break** is shown in mint with a ☕ cup.

**When a session ends**
- You get a **notification** (if you allowed them) or a **pop-up**.
- Studify moves to the next session (focus → break → focus). Press **Start** to begin it.

> The timer keeps accurate time even if you switch tabs. If you close the browser, the timer starts over next time.

## 9. Using Studify on a phone

Studify works in your phone's browser. There's nothing to install.

**Getting started**
1. Open Studify in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone).
2. Tap **Connect Spotify** in the top-right corner and agree (as in [section 3](#3-connecting-your-spotify-account)). If Android jumps into the Spotify app during login and comes back in a new tab, that's fine.
3. Open the **Spotify app**, then switch back to your browser.
4. Pick a record and tap **Play** *(Premium)*, or play something in the Spotify app *(any plan)*.

**Layout:** one column. The player is at the top; scroll down for **Records · Tracks · Lyrics**. Everything works by tapping and swiping:
- **Swipe** up and down on the shelf to browse. **Tap** a record to select it, then tap **Play**.
- **Tap Focus timer** to open the timer, and drag the sliders with your finger.
- To scroll the **page** rather than the shelf, swipe on the headings, tabs or player instead.

**Good to know on phones**
- **The music always plays from the Spotify app** on phones. Spotify's in-browser player doesn't work in phone browsers, so there's no *Play here*.
- **If Studify says to open the Spotify app**, bring the app to the front once. Phones put apps to sleep when they've been in the background for a while.
- **Keep Studify on screen.** Phones pause web pages in the background, so the record and progress freeze while you're in another app. They catch up within a couple of seconds when you come back.
- **The focus timer keeps correct time in the background**, but the end-of-session alert may only appear when you return to Studify.
- Turning the phone sideways works too.

## 10. Keyboard shortcuts

Studify works fully without a mouse. Press **Tab** to move between controls; the focused one gets a coloured outline.

**Anywhere**

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **Space** | Play / pause *(Premium)* |
| **←** / **→** | Previous / next song *(Premium)* |
| **T** | Start / pause the focus timer |

**On the record shelf** (press Tab to reach it first)

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **↑** / **↓** | Previous / next record |
| **Page Up** / **Page Down** | Jump 5 records |
| **Home** / **End** | First / last record |
| **Enter** | Play the selected record *(Premium)* |

**On a song list** (the Tracks tab or **Tracks ›**)

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **↑** / **↓** | Previous / next song |
| **Home** / **End** | First / last song |
| **Enter** | Play that song *(Premium)* |
| **Esc** | Close the **Tracks ›** list |

**In the lyrics:** **↑ / ↓ / Page Up / Page Down** scroll them. Auto-follow pauses, the same as when you scroll with the mouse.

**On any row of tabs** (Records · Tracks · Lyrics, or Playlists · Albums · Liked): **←** / **→** switch tabs.

**In the focus timer panel:** **Esc** closes it, and **← / →** move a selected slider.

> Hover over a button to see its shortcut.

## 11. Accessibility

- **Screen readers:** every button is labelled. Studify announces each new song, and when focus or break time starts.
- **Text size and zoom:** Studify follows your browser's text size and zoom (**Ctrl / ⌘ and +**).
- **Less motion:** turn on *Reduce motion* in your device settings and Studify stops its animations. The record starts and stops instantly, panels don't slide, and the shelf becomes a flat list.
  - Windows: *Settings › Accessibility › Visual effects › Animation effects* (off)
  - macOS: *System Settings › Accessibility › Display › Reduce motion*
  - iPhone: *Settings › Accessibility › Motion › Reduce Motion*
  - Android: *Settings › Accessibility › Remove animations*
- **More contrast:** turn on *Increase contrast* in your device settings, and faint text becomes full-strength.
- **Colour is never the only signal:** focus and break also show a label and an icon.

# Help

## 12. Messages and how to fix them

Messages appear briefly at the bottom of the screen.

**Playing music**

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Open the Spotify app on any device — no need to play anything — then try again.** | No Spotify app is open, or a phone has put it to sleep | Open the Spotify app (bring it to the front on a phone), then press play in Studify again |
| **This account is on Spotify Free — Studify will follow along…** | Your account is Free. Studify has switched to follow mode for the rest of this session | Play, pause and skip in the Spotify app; Studify follows along |
| **Spotify won't allow that right now.** | Spotify blocked that action (for example, skipping during an ad). If it appears for **everything**, you're probably not on the access list yet (see the row below) | Try again in a moment, or ask the person who runs Studify to add you |
| **Couldn't start playing here — try again, or keep using the Spotify app.** | *Play here* couldn't connect | Click **Play here** again, or use the Spotify app |
| **This browser can't play Spotify here — keep using the Spotify app.** | Your browser doesn't support Spotify's in-browser player | Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer, or just the Spotify app |
| **Spotify only shares the songs of playlists you own or collaborate on…** (in a song list) | You only follow this playlist, so Spotify won't list its songs | Play the whole record from the shelf instead |

**Connecting and logging in**

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Your Spotify account isn't on this Studify's access list yet…** | You logged in, but the person who runs Studify hasn't added your Spotify account. Spotify then refuses everything Studify asks for | Send them **the email address of your Spotify account** (spotify.com › Account › Edit profile). Once they've added you, click **● Spotify connected › Disconnect Spotify**, then **Connect Spotify** again |
| **Session expired — please reconnect.** | Your login ran out or was cancelled | Click **Connect Spotify** (top right) |
| **Spotify access was declined.** | You pressed *Cancel* on Spotify's page | Click **Connect Spotify** and choose **Agree** |
| **Login could not be verified — please connect again.** | The login didn't finish properly (for example, an old or copied link) | Click **Connect Spotify** again, and finish in the same browser |
| **Spotify login failed — please try again.** | Something went wrong while logging in | Try again. If it keeps happening, ask the person who runs Studify whether you've been added |
| **Spotify login needs a secure address…** | Studify was opened at an address that can't log in (one starting with `http://192.168…`) | Open Studify at its `https://` address. Ask the person who runs it |
| **Spotify client ID is missing…** | Studify isn't set up yet | For the person running Studify: see the [Setup Guide](./SPOTIFY_SETUP.md) |

**Connection problems**

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Spotify is busy — try again in N s.** | Too many requests in a short time | Wait the number of seconds shown |
| **Can't reach Spotify — check your connection.** | One request failed | Check your internet. Studify tries again by itself |
| **You're offline** (on the player card) and **Offline — showing the last song. Reconnecting automatically…** | The connection dropped, or Studify was opened without internet. You stay logged in | Nothing to do. When you're back online, Studify catches up at once, and reloads anything that failed (shelf, song list, lyrics). Lyrics already on screen keep scrolling |
| **Spotify is having trouble — try again shortly.** | Spotify itself has a problem | Wait a minute |
| **Something went wrong.** | An unexpected problem | Try again. If it keeps happening, tell the person who runs Studify |

**Other things that can happen**
- **Nothing shows even though music is playing.** Check that you're logged into the **same** Spotify account in the app and in Studify. Private sessions and ads aren't shown.
- **The record doesn't spin.** It only spins while music is actually playing. Check the Spotify app isn't paused.
- **Studify still shows "Connect Spotify" after you agreed.** You may not be on Studify's list of allowed people yet. Ask the person who runs it.
- **The colours didn't change for a song.** Some covers can't be read, so Studify keeps its default colours. Nothing is wrong.

## 13. Privacy: what Studify keeps

- Studify runs **entirely in your browser**. There's no Studify server and no Studify account.
- It talks to **Spotify**, and to **LRCLIB** for lyrics. LRCLIB only receives the **song name, artist, album and length** of what's playing, nothing about you.
- It keeps these, **in this browser only**:
  - your Spotify **login key**, so you stay connected;
  - which **permissions** you gave;
  - your **focus and break lengths**;
  - whether **Show lyrics when I play** is on;
  - whether your account turned out to be **Free** (forgotten when you disconnect);
  - **lyrics** of songs you've played, for 30 days, so they appear instantly next time and work offline (lyrics aren't personal: just the song's words);
  - your **record shelf** (playlist, album and liked-song names and covers), so it shows at once when you open Studify. It's **deleted when you disconnect**.
- It never sees your Spotify password.
- **Disconnecting** (next section) deletes the login key and the saved shelf from the browser. To remove saved lyrics as well, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## 14. Disconnecting

1. Click **● Spotify connected** in the top-right corner.
2. Click **Disconnect Spotify**.

To remove Studify's access from your Spotify account completely, go to **spotify.com › Account › Manage apps**, find Studify, and click **Remove access**.

## 15. Questions people ask

**Do I need Premium?**
No. Free accounts can use everything except controlling the music from Studify (see [section 2](#2-free-or-premium-what-works)).

**Do I have to start a song in Spotify first?**
No. With Premium, just have the Spotify app open and press **Play** in Studify.

**Does Studify play the music itself?**
Usually the Spotify app plays it, and Studify shows and controls it. On a computer with Premium, **Play here** plays it inside the Studify tab.

**Why are the lyrics missing or a little off for some songs?**
They come from LRCLIB, a free library written by volunteers. Studify only shows lyrics it's confident belong to the song. If none match, it says so rather than guessing.

**Does it cost anything?**
No. Studify is free and uses your own Spotify account.

**Can I use it on two devices?**
Yes. Connect once in each browser.

**Can I use it with a speaker or smart TV playing Spotify?**
Yes. Studify follows whichever device Spotify is playing on.

**Why can't I see some of my playlists or their songs?**
Each shelf tab shows your first 50. Spotify only shares the song lists of playlists you made or collaborate on, and some playlists made by Spotify itself are closed to apps.

**Is my listening shared with anyone?**
No. Studify has no server, and your data only goes to Spotify (plus the song details sent to LRCLIB for lyrics).
